Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

We want every family to be happy with DynastyVault. This policy applies to payments made to DynastyVault, run by the DynastyVault team, India.

Royal Dynasty Lifetime and add-ons

  • If you paid but did not receive an unlock code within 3 days, you get a full refund on request.
  • If the unlock code does not work and we cannot fix it within 3 days, you get a full refund.
  • Within 7 days of unlocking, if Royal does not work as described on the pricing page, message us — we will fix it or refund you.
  • After 7 days, or once the feature is working as described, payments are not refundable because the digital product has been delivered.

Made-for-you services (reunion photo, voice story, gift box)

  • If we have not started your order, you can cancel for a full refund.
  • If we cannot deliver within 7 days of receiving everything we need, you get a full refund.
  • Each order includes one free redo if a face or the voice does not look or sound right.
  • Once delivered and any redo is done, the order is not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Send your order ID and the UPI transaction ID to us. Approved refunds go back to the same UPI ID or card within 5–7 working days.
