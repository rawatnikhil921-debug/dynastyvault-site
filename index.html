Questions

Things families ask us

Where is my family’s data stored?

In your own browser, on your own device. We don’t keep a copy. Use “1-Click Family Archive” in the app to download a backup, and “Restore” to open it on another phone or laptop.

How does the cloned voice work?

You record about 20 seconds of the person, or upload a voice note or video where they speak. Press Play and the story is read in that voice — included with Royal, or with a Voice Story Pack, on any phone.

Can I use an old voice note of someone who has passed away?

Yes, if the family agrees. Upload the clearest 15–30 seconds you have, with one voice and no music. Please only clone a voice with the family’s permission.

Is the reunion photo real?

The family collage uses everyone’s real photos, unchanged. If you want one photo where everyone stands together, our team makes it with AI and checks every face before sending it.

Can I pay from outside India?

Yes. Outside India the app shows prices in US dollars and a QR that opens a card / PayPal payment — your bank converts it to your own currency. Indians abroad with a UPI app can also pay in rupees by UPI.

How do I pay, and when does Royal unlock?

Scan the QR in the app, then type the transaction ID. We see the payment and send an unlock code on WhatsApp — usually within a few hours. Enter the code and Royal opens on that device.

Which languages are there?

The app works in English, Hinglish and Hindi. With Royal, stories can be translated and read in 15 languages including Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu.
