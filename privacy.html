Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what DynastyVault, run by the DynastyVault team, India, does with information about you and your family.

In short: your family tree, photos, stories and voice recordings stay in your own browser. We only receive what you choose to send us — payment details and, for made-for-you orders, the photos or voice notes you send on WhatsApp.

1. Information stored on your device

Names, dates, photos, stories, voice recordings and map places you add are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. You can delete them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

2. Information you send us

  • Payments: when you pay, you send us your name, WhatsApp number and the UPI transaction ID so we can match your payment. UPI and Razorpay process the payment itself; we never see your card number or UPI PIN.
  • Made-for-you orders: photos, voice notes and stories you send on WhatsApp are used only to make your order. We delete them within 30 days of delivery unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Messages: if you contact us we keep the conversation to help you.

3. Services that process data for features you turn on

  • Voice clone: runs on your own laptop through the DynastyVault voice program. Your recording does not leave your computer.
  • AI story writer: sends the notes you type to Google Gemini using your own Google API key, under Google’s privacy terms.
  • Fonts and maps: the website loads fonts from Google Fonts and may load map tiles from public map providers, which can see your IP address.

4. Children

Families add children to their trees. Please add only what you are comfortable keeping on your device, and do not share a child’s information publicly.

5. Your choices

You can export everything with “1-Click Family Archive”, and delete everything by clearing the site data. To ask us to delete anything you sent us, contact us below.
