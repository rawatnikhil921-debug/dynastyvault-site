Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what DynastyVault, run by the DynastyVault team, India, does with information about you and your family.

In short: your family tree, photos, stories and voice recordings stay in your own browser unless you choose otherwise. If you make a DynastyVault account, your profile and the parts of your family tree you choose to show are stored on our server so other people can see them and you can log in on another phone. We also receive what you send us — payment details and, for made-for-you orders, the photos or voice notes you send on WhatsApp.

1. Information stored on your device

Names, dates, photos, stories, voice recordings and map places you add are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers unless you make an account (see section 2). You can delete them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

2. Your DynastyVault account (optional)

  • What we store: your username, name, password (only as a secure scrambled hash — we cannot read it), optional email, profile photo, bio, city, who you follow and who follows you.
  • Your family tree on your profile: if you turn on “Show my family tree on my profile”, the tree, descriptions, posts and memories you tick are stored on our server. Member photos are shared only if you tick them. A public account can be seen by anyone; a private account only by people you approve.
  • Backup for other phones: while you are logged in, a copy of your family tree (without voice recordings) is saved to your account so it comes back when you log in on another phone.
  • Deleting: “Delete account” in Edit profile removes your profile, follows and backup from our server.
  • Where: our server runs on Render and stores data in a Supabase database.

3. Information you send us

  • Payments: when you pay, you send us your name, WhatsApp number and the UPI transaction ID so we can match your payment. UPI and Razorpay process the payment itself; we never see your card number or UPI PIN.
  • Made-for-you orders: photos, voice notes and stories you send on WhatsApp are used only to make your order. We delete them within 30 days of delivery unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Messages: if you contact us we keep the conversation to help you.

4. Services that process data for features you use

  • Voice clone: runs on your own laptop through the DynastyVault voice program. Your recording does not leave your computer.
  • AI story writer: sends the notes you type to Google Gemini through our server, under Google’s terms.
  • Reunion portraits and family documentaries (Royal): the photos and life story you choose are sent through our server to Google’s AI to make the picture or film. The finished film is kept on your phone.
  • Visitor counter: the website counts visits anonymously with a random ID per phone; we do not store names or IP addresses for this.
  • Fonts and maps: the website loads fonts from Google Fonts and may load map tiles from public map providers, which can see your IP address.

5. Children

Families add children to their trees. Please add only what you are comfortable keeping on your device, and do not share a child’s information publicly.

6. Your choices

You can export everything with “1-Click Family Archive”, and delete everything by clearing the site data. To ask us to delete anything you sent us, contact us below.
