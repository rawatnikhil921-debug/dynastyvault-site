Family tree kaise banayein — dono taraf, aasan tarike se

Iske liye kisi record ya purane kaagaz ki zaroorat nahi. Zyada tar familyan ek shaam mein 3–4 peedhiyan bana leti hain — bas sahi logon se sahi sawaal poochh ke.

1. Apne naam se shuru karo, upar jao

Sabse pehle apna naam likho. Uske upar Papa aur Mummy. Unke upar Dada-Dadi aur Nana-Nani. Yahi har family tree ki neev hai — aur inme se zyada tar naam aapko pehle se pata honge.

💡 Poore naam likho — aur shaadi se pehle ka surname bhi. Nani ka maayke wala naam aksar ek poori nayi family ka darwaza khol deta hai.

2. Sirf Papa ki taraf nahi — Mummy ki taraf bhi

Bahut se family tree sirf Dada ki taraf chalte hain. Par aapki aadhi kahani Nana-Nani ki family se aati hai. Dono taraf saath rakho: left mein Dada-Dadi, right mein Nana-Nani, aur beech mein Mummy-Papa aur aap.

3. Isi hafte buzurgon se baat karo

Sabse achha source aaj bhi Dada-Dadi, Nana-Nani, bua ya chacha hain. Phone karo ya agle family lunch pe poochho:

  • Aapke mummy-papa aur dada-dadi ke naam kya the?
  • Aap kahan paida hue, aur usse pehle family kahan rehti thi?
  • Kaun kab aur kyun shehar badla?
  • Aapke kitne bhai-behen the? Unki shaadi kisse hui?
  • Family mein log kya kaam karte the?
🎙️ Baat-cheet phone pe record kar lo. Saalon baad unki awaaz utni hi keemti hogi jitni jaankari.

4. Tareekh aur jagah likho — andaaza bhi chalega

“Lagbhag 1950” ya “Lahore ke paas ek gaon” — kuch na hone se bahut behtar hai. Jagahon se aap family ka safar (migration) dekh paoge — aur zyada tar log hairaan hote hain ki family kitni door se aayi.

5. Photos, kahaniyan aur awaaz jodo

Sirf naamon ki list ek tree hai; photo aur kahani usse family banati hai. Purani photos phone se scan karo, har insaan ke baare mein 2–3 line likho, aur buzurgon ki ek chhoti voice note save kar lo.

6. Family group mein share karo

Tree ko family WhatsApp group mein bhejo. Cousins woh naam jodenge jo aapko pata hi nahi the, aur kisi na kisi ke paas woh photo hogi jo missing thi. Family tree kabhi khatam nahi hota — har baar khulne pe badhta hai.
